# GDP-per-Country-2020-2025
Country-level GDP (2020–2025), Annual Economic Data


![image](https://github.com/Ishita95-harvard/GDP-per-Country-2020-2025/blob/main/dataset-cover.png)

### Data Analysis Key Findings
- The mean economic value across all countries showed a consistent upward trend from 2020 ($1,289,309.11) to 2025 ($1,441,600.97).
- The United States consistently held the highest economic value throughout the period (2020-2025), with values ranging from $20,937
  billion in 2020 to $28,779 billion in 2025.
- Tuvalu consistently recorded the lowest economic value from 2020 to 2025, with values around $54 million each year.
- Guyana experienced the highest growth rate between 2020 and 2023, at 209.25%.
- Laos showed the lowest growth rate, with a decline of -19.09% between 2020 and 2023.
### Insights
- Investigate the factors contributing to Guyana's exceptionally high growth rate between 2020 and 2023.
- Explore potential reasons for Laos's negative growth rate during the same period and its economic challenges.
